How PricePoa Works

Three-step price comparison process

No application download, no account registration, and no change to your existing WhatsApp usage required.

01

Submit your price request

02

We retrieve current prices

PricePoa queries real-time pricing data from our retail partners and local dukas in your area to provide up-to-date cost information.

03

You receive a clear, itemized comparison showing prices from each store, highlighting the most cost-effective option for your entire basket.
