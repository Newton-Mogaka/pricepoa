Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about PricePoa

Find concise answers to frequently asked questions regarding our price comparison service.

Is there any cost to use PricePoa?

+

PricePoa is provided free of charge to all users. There are no subscription fees, hidden charges, or costs of any kind associated with using our price comparison service. We sustain operations through anonymized, aggregated data insights that assist retail partners in optimizing their pricing strategies.

How frequently is pricing data updated?

+

Pricing data is refreshed multiple times each day to ensure accuracy and relevance. We prioritize updates during peak shopping hours and continuously monitor for price changes at our partner stores. While we strive to provide the most current information available, we recommend verifying prices at the point of purchase as a final confirmation step.

Which geographic areas does PricePoa currently serve?

+

PricePoa currently focuses on Nairobi and its surrounding regions, with strong coverage in Kasarani, Ruiru, Thika, and along the Mombasa Road corridor. We are actively expanding our service to other major Kenyan cities including Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and Eldoret. Local dukas (neighborhood shops) are included based on the general location provided in your request.

Is it necessary to create an account to use PricePoa?

+

No account creation or registration is required. PricePoa functions directly through your existing WhatsApp application. Simply message our official number and begin requesting price comparisons immediately—no registration, login, or personal information submission is necessary.

How does PricePoa protect user privacy and data security?

+

PricePoa leverages WhatsApp's inherent end-to-end encryption, ensuring that all messages remain secure from interception. We do not store your phone number, WhatsApp identifier, price comparison requests, or any personal data. All processing occurs in real-time, and we follow strict data minimization principles—utilizing only the information essential to deliver the price comparison service.
