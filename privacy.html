How PricePoa Ensures Privacy

PricePoa operates exclusively within WhatsApp's secure infrastructure. When you submit a price comparison request:

  • Your message remains within WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted channel
  • PricePoa processes your request in real-time without retaining personal data
  • We obtain current pricing from partner stores through anonymized, aggregated queries
  • Results are delivered to you via the same secure WhatsApp connection
  • No phone numbers, shopping lists, or personal identifiers are stored after the session concludes

Data Protection Principles

PricePoa does not:

  • Retain your phone number or WhatsApp identifier
  • Keep records of your price comparison requests or shopping lists
  • Share user data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes
  • Employ tracking mechanisms or analytics that could identify individual users

PricePoa does:

  • Process price comparison requests instantly and deliver results within seconds
  • Utilize aggregated, anonymized data solely for service improvement and partner insights
  • Adhere to WhatsApp's Business Policies and align with GDPR principles regarding data minimization
  • Offer transparent service with clear information about data usage and user rights
Privacy Policy

Our commitment to data protection and user privacy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Policy Overview

PricePoa ("we", "our", or "us") operates the PricePoa website and WhatsApp-based price comparison service. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information in connection with your use of our service.

By accessing or using our service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this policy carefully to understand our commitment to protecting your privacy.

Information Practices

PricePoa adheres to strict data minimization principles, collecting only the information essential to provide our price comparison service:

  • User-Submitted Queries: The textual content of price comparison requests transmitted via WhatsApp (for example, "maize flour 2kg, sugar 1kg")
  • Service Metrics: Anonymous, aggregated statistical data concerning service utilization (query frequency, popular items, average user savings)
  • Website Administration Data: Standard server logs facilitating website operations and security monitoring (IP addresses, browser characteristics, referral URLs)

Information We Do Not Collect:

  • Personal contact information including telephone numbers or WhatsApp identifiers
  • Personal identifying information such as full names, physical addresses, or email addresses
  • Precise geolocation data beyond the general service area required for store matching
  • Historical shopping baskets, purchase records, or transaction histories
  • Financial information, payment details, or banking data

Purpose and Use of Information

The limited information we obtain is utilized exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Processing price comparison requests with minimal latency to provide timely responses
  • Generating accurate, current price information from our authorized retail partners
  • Enhancing service effectiveness and user experience through analysis of anonymized, aggregated usage patterns
  • Maintaining platform security, integrity, and operational reliability
  • Distributing service-related notifications exclusively through authorized channels when users have elected to receive such communications

Information Sharing Practices

PricePoa maintains a strict prohibition against the sale, exchange, or unauthorized distribution of personally identifiable information to external entities. Authorized sharing scenarios are limited to:

We may share aggregated, anonymized datasets with:

  • Our authorized retail partners for internal market analysis and inventory optimization (containing no personal identifiers)
  • Academic and research institutions conducting studies on consumer purchasing patterns and pricing sensitivity (using strictly anonymized datasets)
  • Government and regulatory bodies when disclosure is legally mandated or required for compliance purposes

Information Security Measures

PricePoa implements comprehensive technical and administrative safeguards to protect user information:

  • Utilizing WhatsApp's inherent end-to-end encryption protocol to secure all message transmissions against interception
  • Maintaining stringent access control systems governing who may interact with any data processing systems
  • Applying regular security updates and patch management to all technological infrastructure
  • Conducting systematic security evaluations and vulnerability assessments at defined intervals
  • Providing ongoing data protection and privacy training for all personnel involved in service operations

While PricePoa employs industry-standard security measures and follows recognized best practices for data protection, users should recognize that no security system can provide absolute guarantees against all potential threats or vulnerabilities.

User Rights and Choices

In accordance with applicable data protection regulations, users may possess certain rights concerning their personal information:

  • Right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information maintained by PricePoa
  • Right to object to or impose limitations on specific uses of their personal information
  • Right to obtain a portable copy of their personal data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to revoke previously granted consent for data processing activities, where legally applicable

To initiate a request concerning your privacy rights or to submit inquiries about our privacy practices, please contact us using the information provided below.

Children's Online Privacy

PricePoa's service is not designed for, nor does it knowingly collect personal information from, individuals under the age of thirteen (13).

If a parent or legal guardian becomes aware that a child under 13 has somehow provided personal information to PricePoa, we request immediate notification so we may undertake prompt remedial measures in accordance with applicable children's privacy regulations.

Policy Updates and Version Control

PricePoa reserves the right to modify, update, or amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technological developments, or regulatory requirements.

Notification of material changes to this policy will be provided through prominent announcement on this webpage, accompanied by revision of the "Last updated" timestamp displayed above.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy on a regular basis to maintain awareness of our current privacy practices and any modifications that may have been implemented.
