The Technology Behind PricePoa

Natural Language Processing

Our advanced NLP engine understands your requests in English, Sheng, or mixed languages, extracting items, quantities, and contextual clues accurately.

Real-time Price Engine

Our proprietary price-checking system queries multiple data sources including store APIs, web scraping (where permitted), and partner feeds to ensure current pricing.

Smart Matching Algorithm

Our matching engine compares apples-to-apples by normalizing product names, brands, sizes, and variants to ensure accurate comparisons.

Delivery System

Results are formatted for optimal WhatsApp reading and delivered through secure, encrypted channels directly to your phone.

Our Data Sources

PricePoa gets pricing information from multiple reliable sources to ensure accuracy and completeness:

Partner Stores

Direct integration with Naivas, Carrefour, Quickmart, and Chandarana for real-time shelf prices.

Local Dukas Network

Aggregated pricing from thousands of local dukas across Nairobi and expanding regions.

Market Feeds

Authorized market data feeds providing wholesale and retail pricing trends.

User-Verified Updates

Community-powered price verification system helping us maintain accuracy.
