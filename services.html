More Ways to Compare & Contribute

Beyond text-based requests, PricePoa offers powerful visual tools for live shopping and community-driven data enrichment.

Photo Price Lookup

Snap a photo of any product on the shelf while shopping. Our AI identifies the item instantly and returns real-time price comparisons across Naivas, Carrefour, Quickmart, Chandarana, and nearby dukas. No typing needed—just point, shoot, and save.

Receipt Scanning & Contribution

After shopping, scan your receipt to automatically log what you bought and at what price. Your anonymized receipt data helps improve price accuracy for everyone, detects promotional pricing, and strengthens the community price database—all while keeping your identity completely private.

Our Data Sources

PricePoa gets pricing information from multiple reliable sources to ensure accuracy and completeness:

Partner Stores

Direct integration with Naivas, Carrefour, Quickmart, and Chandarana for real-time shelf prices.

Aggregated pricing from thousands of local dukas across Nairobi and expanding regions.

Market Feeds

Authorized market data feeds providing wholesale and retail pricing trends.

User-Verified Updates

Community-powered price verification system helping us maintain accuracy.

Business Intelligence & B2B Services

Beyond consumer price comparison, PricePoa transforms aggregated, anonymized shopping data into actionable market intelligence for manufacturers, distributors, and retailers across Kenya.

Shopping Trend Analysis

Identify seasonal demand shifts, category growth trends, and emerging consumer preferences across Nairobi and expanding regions. Plan inventory and promotions with real market signals.

Product Performance Benchmarking

Track how your SKUs perform against competitors across Naivas, Carrefour, Quickmart, Chandarana, and dukas. Measure price elasticity, share of shelf, and promotional effectiveness.

Retail Price Compliance Monitoring

Ensure your recommended retail prices (RRPs) are honored across all channels. Detect price violations, unauthorized discounting, and channel conflict in real time.

Market Entry & Expansion Intelligence

Evaluate new categories, regions, or retail partnerships with granular pricing and demand data. Reduce launch risk with evidence-based go-to-market strategies.

Data Privacy & Ethics

All business intelligence is derived from fully anonymized, aggregated data. Individual user identities, phone numbers, shopping lists, and location data are never shared, sold, or exposed. Our insights reflect market-level patterns only—protecting consumer privacy while enabling smarter business decisions.

Privacy and Security First

Your privacy is our top priority. Here's how we protect your information:

End-to-End Encryption

All communications happen through WhatsApp's secure, encrypted channel.

Zero Data Storage

We don't store your phone number, shopping lists, or personal data.

Anonymized Processing

Price checks use anonymized, aggregated queries that can't be traced back to you.

GDPR Compliant

Our practices align with GDPR principles for data minimization and user rights.
